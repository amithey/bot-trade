Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

Account and billing information necessary to run your subscription (handled by our payment processor, Paddle — we do not store your card details ourselves), and usage data needed to operate the product: your trading profile, watchlist, and paper-trading history.

Your Anthropic API key

If you supply your own Anthropic API key to unlock AI-driven modes, it is held in your session only, used to authenticate your own requests to Anthropic, and is never written to disk or logged.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't share your trading data with third parties except the infrastructure providers necessary to run the service (such as our payment processor and hosting provider).

Your rights

You may request a copy of your data or its deletion at any time by contacting us.
