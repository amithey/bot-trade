Good to know

BotTrade is a paper-trading tool. It does not connect to a brokerage, execute real trades, or manage real funds — every position is virtual. Nothing in this product constitutes financial or investment advice. AI-driven modes run on Anthropic's Claude models via the Anthropic API; when you supply your own API key, usage bills directly to your Anthropic account. Subscription plans grant access to the BotTrade software and its strategy modes.
