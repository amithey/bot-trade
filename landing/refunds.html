Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026

If you're not satisfied with a BotTrade subscription within the first 7 days of a new charge, contact us and we'll issue a full refund — no questions asked.

After 7 days, subscriptions are billed for the period they cover and are non-refundable for that period, but you can cancel at any time to stop future charges; your access continues through the end of the period already paid for.

To request a refund or cancel, email support@bottrade.app.
